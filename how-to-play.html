GETTING STARTED

How to Play

Follow these steps to get everything installed and join the Capital Express server.

01

Download CurseForge

Download and install CurseForge to get started with the CapitalExpress modpack.

Download CurseForge
02

Download Mod Pack

Download the CapitalExpress mod pack and install it through CurseForge.

Download Mod Pack
03

Visit Gameplay page

Visit the Gameplay page to learn how to get started.

Gameplay page
04

Adjust the Resource Pack

Before joining the server click "Options" and then "Resource Pack". On the left hand side you should see "Pretty Realistic" and "High Contrast". Please add them to "Selected" by clicking Play button on their picture.

05

Join the Server

Launch Minecraft with the modpack installed and join the Capital Express server.
