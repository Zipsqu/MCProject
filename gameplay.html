Capital Express Live

Gameplay

Learn the server mechanics!

Each kingdom has it's colour & land (protected from other players). While all the kingdoms start with nothing, future bonuses will be kingdom-relevant. You can exchange your riches for other resources at the server's island. You can also recruit/request all kinds of NPCs (workers,guards,companions) for your island!

Find Your Allegiance

Choose Your Kingdom

Build your empire any way you want! Manufacturing drugs can be profitable but it can bring the attention of cartel or police. Other vices like guns & ammo require constant supply of rare resources. Establish your trading routes with plenty of transport available & create a busy empire with all kinds of NPCs available.
