GETTING STARTED

How to Play

Follow these steps to get everything installed and join the Capital Express server.

01

Download CurseForge

Download and install CurseForge to get started with the CapitalExpress modpack.

Download CurseForge
02

Download Mod Pack

Download the CapitalExpress mod pack and install it through CurseForge.

Download Mod Pack
03

Adjust the Resource Pack

01

Open Options, then select Resource Packs.

02

On the left, find Pretty Realistic and High Contrast.

03

Click the play button on each pack to move it into Selected.

04

Click Done to apply the resource packs.

04

Adjust the Shaders

01

Open Options and select Video Settings.

02

Under Iris, select Shader Packs on the left.

03

Select ComplementaryReimagined, then click Apply.

04

Before exiting, check that the menu says Shaders: Enabled.

05

If you experience graphics issues, press R in-game to refresh.

05

Join the Server

Launch Minecraft with the modpack installed and join the Capital Express server.
