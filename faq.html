FAQ

Answers to some of the most common questions about MC Project, the modpack, and getting started.

I've logged in, and blocks are loading very slowly. What should I do?

If you've just logged in for the first time, the world needs to load locally on your computer. This can make things feel slow initially. Once the world has loaded and cached, things should become noticeably faster the next time you sign in or after you've played for a while.

What are the most optimal settings?

On the About page, press Ctrl+A to select everything, then copy and paste it into ChatGPT. It will recommend the most optimal settings for you. There are quite a few settings to play around with if you want to experiment.

Do I need Minecraft to play on this server?

Yes. You need a legitimate copy of Minecraft Java Edition to play on the server.

How do I learn more about the mods?

Select the mod you're interested in from the About page, then look it up on Google or Modrinth. Most of the mods have plenty of shortcuts, useful tips, and quick references available online.

How do I run the modpack?

Refer to the How To Play. First, download Modrinth, open it, and sign in with your Minecraft account. Then, download the modpack. This will create an instance in your Modrinth app. Once the instance has been created, simply press Play to launch it. After loading in, go to Multiplayer and join the server.
