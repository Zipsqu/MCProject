FAQ

Answers to some of the most common questions about Capital Express, the modpack, and getting started.

The game is slow & I can't see very far. What should I do?

If you've just logged in for the first time, the world needs to save locally on your computer. This can make things feel slow initially. Once the world has loaded and cached, things should become noticeably faster the next time you sign in or after you've played for a while.

How do I get whitelisted to join the server?

Access the Gameplay page first and then contact the admin regarding claiming the kingdom.

Do I need Minecraft to play on this server?

Yes. You need a legitimate copy of Minecraft Java Edition to play on the server.

Can I pick my own resource pack & shaders?

Yes, the server is not forcing a resource pack or shaders - you can pick whatever you like, as long as you PC can handle it.

I've updated/changed the mod pack, how do I fix it?

If you updated or changed the mod pack and now the server is throwing errors, it's best to remove it and download it again from How to Play page.

My PC is struggling, what's the biggest downgrade I can make?

If you just joined the server for the first time, give it some time as world generation can be quite heavy on your PC. Otherwise, while it's not recommended (due to shitty graphics) you can disable Iris Shaders from "Video Settnigs" options.

How do I run the modpack or suggest changes?

Refer to the How To Play page. If you find any mods you like, you can let us know & we wil have a look at the compatibility!
