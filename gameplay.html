Gameplay

Learn the server mechanics!

-Each kingdom has it's colour & land (protected from other players).
-Ivory Kingdom (owned by server) acts as a social/economy hub.
-At the Ivory Dockyard, you can exchange your resources for other minerals, drugs, guns or troops.

Find Your Allegiance

Choose Your Kingdom

-There are many ways to build your empire, whether through farming, industry, transport, warfare or hospitality.
-The key is to balance all of the factors to benefit your kingdom the most.
-As your influence and wealth grow, you may attract attention from the police, cartels, and rival factions (depending on your gameplay).
-You may need to defend your empire on certain ocasions.
